## Post a job

From **Jobs** or **Creator Hub**, create a listing with:

- Title and full scope
- Budget in USDC and category
- Deadline and number of open positions
- Payment structure (single, milestones, split, or initial + rest)

Applications arrive with a pitch and open a **message thread** with each applicant.

## Manage applications

1. Open your listing from Creator Hub
2. Read pitches and chat in each thread
3. **Shortlist** strong candidates
4. **Accept** to create a **job contract** and notify the worker
5. Hire multiple workers from one post until all slots are filled

## Fund and release

On the job contract workspace:

1. Fund the current milestone (balance, creator wallet, or connected wallet)
2. Wait for delivery
3. Release payment for that milestone or request changes via chat
4. Repeat for multi-milestone contracts
5. Rate the worker when each milestone or the full contract completes

See [Job board (workers)](/earn/job-board) for the worker-side flow and [Reviews](/earn/reviews) for rating rules.

## Fees

Employer fee, worker fee, deposit amount, and net to worker are shown on the contract page before you fund.
