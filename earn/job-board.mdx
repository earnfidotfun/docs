## Overview

The **Job Board** (`/jobs`) is for longer freelance-style work with USDC escrow. Posters publish briefs; workers apply with a pitch and get hired into a **job contract**.

## For workers

1. Open **Jobs** and browse listings (search and category filters)
2. Read scope, budget, deadline, and poster profile
3. **Apply** with a short pitch (opens a message thread with the poster)
4. Track status: pending, accepted, or rejected
5. When hired, open your **job contract** workspace to deliver and get paid

## For hirers

1. **Post a job**: title, scope, budget (USDC), category, deadline, open positions
2. Choose a **payment structure** (see below)
3. Review applications and pitches in **Creator Hub**
4. **Shortlist** strong applicants and **accept** to spawn a protected contract
5. Multi-slot posts let you hire several workers; the listing closes when all slots are filled

## Payment structures

| Structure | How it works |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Single payment** | Fund the full amount, worker delivers, you release once |
| **Milestones** | Define milestones that sum to the budget; fund and release one at a time |
| **Split** | Pay across defined chunks on a schedule |
| **Initial + rest** | Upfront chunk, then remainder on completion |

Milestone contracts only lock escrow for the **current** milestone unless you choose single payment upfront.

## Job contract workspace

After hire, both parties use `/job-contract/{slug}`:

1. Employer **funds** the current milestone (balance, creator wallet, or connected wallet)
2. Worker **delivers** with a delivery note
3. Employer **reviews** and **releases** for that milestone
4. Repeat until all milestones are done
5. **Rate each other** (per milestone and for the full contract when finished)
6. Use **in-contract chat** on the work thread

Fees (employer fee, worker fee, net to worker) are shown on the contract page.

## Ratings

Job contract and milestone ratings feed your public profile and help you win the next gig. See [Reviews & reputation](/earn/reviews).
