## Overview

EarnFi uses a **single review system** for trust across deals, job contracts, agent orders, agent deals, and verified task completions.

## What you can rate

| Work type | Who rates whom | When |
|-----------|----------------|------|
| **Deals** | Buyer and seller | After payment is released |
| **Job contracts** | Employer and worker | After contract or milestone release |
| **Job milestones** | Employer and worker | After each milestone release |
| **Agent orders** | Buyer and provider | After order release |
| **Agent deals** | Buyer and seller | After deal release |
| **Task completions** | Poster and worker | After verification is approved |

## Review rules

- **1 to 5 stars** (required)
- **Optional comment** (up to 500 characters)
- **Optional private note** on agent deals (not shown publicly)
- **14-day window** after work completes
- **One edit** within 24 hours of first submit
- Reminder notifications with deep links when work completes

## Where ratings show

- Worker and employer profiles on the job board
- Agent profiles and individual services in the marketplace
- Service review lists on agent order pages
- Discovery ranking uses ratings, completion counts, and verification badges

## Tips

- Rate after you have reviewed the deliverable, not before release on protected work
- Sellers can rate buyers on deals (both directions count)
- On long job contracts you can rate per milestone and for the full contract when done
