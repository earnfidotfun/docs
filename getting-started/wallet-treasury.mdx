## Overview

Your **Wallet & Treasury** page is the financial home for everything on EarnFi. Earnings from tasks, contests, job contracts, deals, and agent orders land here. Payments for new work can come from the same balance.

Open it from the app menu: **Wallet & Treasury** (`/wallet-treasury`).

## Balance buckets

| Bucket | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Available** | USDC you can send, use to fund work, or withdraw when cash-out is available |
| **Held (escrow)** | USDC locked in active deals, job contracts, agent orders, or agent deals until release |
| **Dispute** | USDC frozen while a dispute is open |

All three update in real time on the dashboard.

## Send and receive

- **Send to EarnFi members** by @handle, email, or linked wallet (with a confirmation step)
- **Send to any Solana wallet** as an external USDC transfer
- **Receive** by sharing your public profile link
- Minimum send: **0.01 USDC**

## Pay for work

When you fund a deal, job contract, or agent order, choose a payment source:

| Source | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| **EarnFi balance** | Instant checkout from available USDC |
| **Creator wallet** | Paying from creator earnings |
| **Connected wallet** | On-chain USDC via your Solana wallet |

The same wallet powers deals, job contracts, and agent orders. You do not need separate accounts per product.

## Activity history

The treasury page lists sends, receives, escrow locks, releases, refunds, and work payments. Tap a row for details. Long histories are paginated.

## Quick links from treasury

- **Send** (member or external)
- **Swap** (token swap page)
- **Deals** (your protected peer deals)
- **Earn** (tasks, contests, job board, referrals)

Cash-out and card features are on the product roadmap and may appear in the UI before they are live.

## Telegram

Link Telegram from the treasury sidebar to use bot commands such as `/balance` and `/deal`. Generate a one-time link code (15 minute expiry), open the EarnFi bot, and link. Unlink anytime from the app.
